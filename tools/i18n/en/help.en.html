Goal — Gather clues to discoveries in libraries, taverns and trading posts, then propose a voyage to a patron at a palace or mansion. Sign a contract, find the destination before the deadline and report back to earn a reward and fame. As your fame grows you will meet greater patrons and companions.
Towns — Pick a building from the menu on the right. Menus and choices move with ↑↓ by default, Enter/Space selects and Esc closes. Harbor: set sail, supplies, fleet / Trading post: buy, sell, haggle / Shipyard: buy, repair and refit ships / Tavern: hire sailors, rumors, companions, barmaids, poker / Inn: rest and save / Town gate: overland expeditions.
Sailing — Click the sea and your fleet finds a route there. Click a town marker to head for that port; you dock when you arrive.
  Arrow keys: while held, the bow slowly turns toward that heading — ← west · → east · ↑ north · ↓ south; press two keys together for diagonals such as northwest or southeast. Release to sail straight on (slower against the wind). Press Space (default) to furl the sails and stop (stopping beside a port anchors you; if you stopped while on autopilot or a set course, press again to resume). The numpad keys 1–9 also set one of eight headings.
  The old manual helm (hold ↑ to move, ←→ to steer the bow) can be chosen in Notebook → Settings → "Sea arrow keys".
  F11: toggle full screen (this is the game's own full screen, so the browser menu will not drop down when the mouse touches the top edge)
  Default 1/2/3 sailing speed · M sea chart · Enter dock · mouse wheel zoom · right-click cancels the course. Direction, confirm, cancel and sea-speed keys can all be changed in Notebook → Settings → "Keyboard".
  Sailing into the wind is slow. Autopilot and set courses beat straight upwind (in Settings → "Upwind zigzag" you can switch to tacking left and right, on the open sea only or always — you arrive on the same day). When steering by hand you must tack yourself. Lateen sails are better upwind, square sails downwind. When food and water run out, your sailors start to collapse.
Landing & exploring — Land near a coast or set out from a town gate. All your sailors join the expedition. Click the ground to walk, look for ruins and wildlife, and enter inland towns. Give gifts to the locals and they share food and water, sometimes more, or let you sleep by their campfire (fatigue recovers as at an inn).
Relics — At a discovery site you may find treasure, jewelry, weapons, books, figureheads and more. They are proof of the discovery: report to your patron and you hand them over (books and items that lead to the next expedition may be returned), or announce the find yourself at a port and they are yours to sell. Reading a book from your belongings reveals new clues.
Land battles — Split the expedition into the admiral's unit and troops (infantry, cavalry, musketeers, artillery). 1 attack · 2 defend · 3–6 special tactics (ambush, traps, snipers, shells). Click an enemy unit to target it — cavalry can reach the leader in the back row. Defeat the leader to win; lose the admiral's unit and you lose.
Sea battles — Steer the flagship with the arrow keys and stop with Space (default). Click the sea to move there; click an enemy ship to close in and attack. Broadsides fire only when your side faces the enemy (ships in your firing arc are fired on automatically). Close in and press B to board. P pauses. Every ship is numbered and No. 1 is the flagship — sink or capture the enemy flagship to win; lose yours and you lose.
Duels — Two fighters face off on a deck (or in a tavern). First choose a stance (1 all-out charge · 2 power attack · 3 take alive · 4 defensive) and blows are traded according to strength and swordsmanship. Each exchange you may pick the next move (0 auto · 1 slash · 2 thrust · 3 strike); thrust beats slash, slash beats strike, strike beats thrust (better swordsmanship reads the opponent's move more often). When your momentum fills, use a technique (killing blow, first strike, flank, capture, feigned retreat, secret weapon, lure, switch, persuade, bluff, war cry, finisher). Space pauses · Esc backs off.
Saving — The game autosaves every minute during play; when all 5 slots are full the oldest is overwritten. The inn's "Services" menu has 5 separate journal slots.
